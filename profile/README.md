# United Clan RP

A Dutch Minecraft building server (Java Edition) for players from the Netherlands and Belgium.

United Clan RP is about community and building something together. No hardcore, no PvP grind, no forced roleplay: just a good world, friendly people and room to create. Each season starts on a fresh world with custom world generation, and builds from earlier seasons find their way back into it.

## What we build

Almost everything on the server is made in-house:

- **Server plugins** for Paper and Velocity: clans and claims, chest locks, staff tools, a season border, custom recipes made by players, and more.
- **A Discord bot** for whitelist applications, tickets and moderation.
- **A website** with information about the server (coming soon).

These repositories are private.

## Next season

The next season opens in early 2027. Follow the announcements on our Discord.
